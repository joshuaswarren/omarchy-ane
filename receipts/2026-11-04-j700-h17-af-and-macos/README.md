## Development and evidence corrections

The prior full-probe C9-C11 "registers" at `0x1137xxx` were descriptor
references/file offsets. Their zero readback does not characterize AF
configuration registers. The prior tunable probe mapped `0x1984000` as a
physical address instead of engine-relative; its freeze cannot be used
to diagnose the ANE MCW block. Both modules now refuse at init, before
driver registration or hardware access, retaining historical bodies only
for receipt comparison. There is no opt-in bypass of the new refusal.

Do not assume a status/acknowledgement register must read back its written
value: the C8 value clearing is not by itself a dropped-write diagnosis.
Likewise an absent AFTER log bounds an interval including the write,
readback and logging; it does not isolate one instruction conclusively.

Next implementation prerequisite: finish the corrected descriptor decoder
and review the selected online HAL path against the correct artifact,
including read/ack semantics and timeout stop conditions. The old full
probe is not a valid implementation of that path. The capture reinforces
the family/resource identities but does not supply missing gate-register
addresses; no guessed clock writes are justified by either result.
