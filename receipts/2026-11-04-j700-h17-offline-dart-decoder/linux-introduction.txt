    iommu/apple-dart: Support T8140 DARTs and firmware-owned stream roots

    The DARTs of the A18 Pro (T8140) are T8110-family instances that report
    version 2.2 or later in PARAMS3. At least the AOP DART is locked by the
    bootloader (PROTECT reads back 0x1e7), and its stream roots already
    hold the co-processor firmware's own mappings when Linux takes over.
    The driver handled neither:

     - Attaching a domain to a locked stream copied Linux's whole L1 table
       over the firmware's root table, and releasing it zeroed the table,
       destroying the firmware's mappings.
     - From version 2.2 on, per-stream TLB flush commands take an extra
       command bit, faults are reported through per-stream exception
       records instead of the legacy error registers, and exceptions latched
       before Linux took over are still pending at probe.

    Handle both, and add the device tree inputs the T8140 device tree
    needs:

     - Locked roots: publish Linux's L1 entries only into slots that are
       vacant or that Linux itself filled, keep a shadow copy of what was
       published, and on release clear only entries that still hold
       Linux's value. A slot whose owner has changed fails the operation
       with -EBUSY instead of being overwritten. Publishing is done under
       the DART lock, and errors now propagate out of the attach path.
     - TLB maintenance: wait for the busy bit before and after every T8110
       TLB command, for up to 2 ms instead of 100 us. From version 2.2 on,
       set bit 15 in per-stream flush commands, and after a map or unmap
       flush a locked stream by range instead of invalidating it whole;
       TLB_START and TLB_END take the first and last page number, derived
       from the DART page size, shifted left by two.
     - Faults from version 2.2 on: decode the pending-exception bitmap at
       0x4000 (one bit per stream, write 1 to clear) and the per-stream
       records at 0x8000 + 0x40 * sid (status, access type and fault
       address), plus the block-level summary at 0x704 on version 2.3.
       Drain exceptions left over from before probe, logging them at debug
       level. If the DART is set to lock on error (DIAG_LOCK bit 1), unlock
       it with TLB command 4 once its exceptions have been acknowledged.
     - apple,remap-streams: validate the property at probe and program the
       remaps into the source streams' TCRs when the DART is reset.
     - apple,dma-range on a consumer node: use that window as the domain's
       aperture instead of the DART's, so that each consumer's IOVAs land
       in root slots that are free for it. Consumer and DART windows must
       have a nonzero size, must not overflow or cross the DART's input
       address width, and must be aligned to the DART page size.
     - Power the DART with pm_runtime_resume_and_get() and handle failure
       instead of warning, both when flushing and when releasing a consumer
       of a locked DART. When a locked DART's domain is freed, its entries
       are retired and the stream invalidated first; if the DART cannot be
       powered or the invalidation fails, the page tables are intentionally
       leaked rather than freed while the hardware may still cache them.
     - Return -EINVAL instead of warning when a locked DART is attached with
       anything other than a single stream.

    The probe banner and the DMA range and IAS messages move to debug
    level.
